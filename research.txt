Architecture (Advanced AFML):
Phase 1: Primary Model (The Oracle)
- Data: Binance 1m OHLCV.
- Role: Predict the raw mathematical probability of BTC going UP in exactly 5 minutes.
- Target: 1 (Close_t+5 > Close_t), 0 (otherwise).

Phase 2: Meta-Labeling (Risk & Sizing)
- Data: Polymarket Order Book (YES/NO share prices) + Primary Model Probability.
- Role: Compare the model's predicted probability against the market's implied probability (Share price). 
- Target: 1 if Expected Value is positive AND trade executes profitably as Maker, 0 otherwise.
