LAYER 0 : REGIME DETECTOR
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Research:
- Purpose: Macro filter deciding which strategy (if any) is allowed to trade
  at any given time. Not a strategy itself — an allocation layer.
- Hypothesis: BTC realized volatility captures systemic stress independently
  of ETH's own price action; ETH's own Hurst/ADX captures whether the
  DeFi/L2/altcoin ecosystem it anchors is currently mean-reverting or trending
  — a more relevant proxy for AAVE/ETH-style pairs than BTC alone.

Data:
- Market Data: BTC/USDT & ETH/USDT OHLCV 1h (Binance Futures).
- No alternative/macro data — deliberately kept minimal for reliability and
  low API weight cost (critical after hitting Binance rate-limit bans).

Features:
- Realized Volatility (24h rolling, annualized) on BTC.
- Hurst Exponent (100h rolling window) on ETH — <0.5 signals mean-reversion.
- ADX (14-period) on ETH — trend strength confirmation.

Training:
- No ML — rule-based classifier. Thresholds calibrated via percentile
  analysis on 1 year of hourly data (P25/P50/P75 of each indicator) rather
  than arbitrary values.
- Final thresholds: BTC vol > 0.60 → HIGH_VOL | ETH Hurst > 0.47 & ADX > 28
  → TRENDING | ETH Hurst < 0.43 & ADX < 36 → MEAN_REV | else NEUTRAL.

Backtest:
- Walk-forward validation across 3 distinct 4-month periods (including a
  -38% BTC drawdown period) rather than a single in-sample fit.
- Result: Hurst separation between MEAN_REV and TRENDING regimes stayed at
  0.21–0.23 across all 3 periods — confirms the classifier generalizes
  across market direction, not just the period it was calibrated on.
- Regime distribution close to design target: MEAN_REV 32%, TRENDING 19%,
  HIGH_VOL 12%, NEUTRAL 37%.

Deployment:
- Standalone background thread, recomputes hourly, writes state to a shared
  JSON + Supabase table consumed by both downstream strategies.

Oversight:
- Live regime label displayed on the public dashboard in real time.
- A live divergence event (AAVE/ETH trending out of the tradable Hurst range
  post-deployment) was correctly detected and used to suppress new StatArb
  entries — validated the filter in a non-simulated setting, not just backtest.

LAYER 1 : STATISTICAL ARBITRAGE (Pairs Trading) — active in MEAN_REV
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Research:
- Concept: Pairs trading — trade the mean-reverting spread between two
  cointegrated assets instead of predicting absolute price direction.
  Zero directional market exposure.
- Pair candidate universe: DeFi/L1/L2 tokens vs ETH (economic rationale:
  shared sector beta), plus BTC/ETH as a macro reference pair.

Data:
- Market Data: AAVE/USDT & ETH/USDT OHLCV 15m (180-day rolling history,
  Binance).

Features:
- Cointegration spread Z-Score (200-candle rolling mean/std).
- Z-Score momentum (1h & 4h derivatives).
- Directional returns (1h) & realized volatility (4h) on both legs.
- Dynamic hedge ratio via Rolling OLS (200-candle window) — replaces a naive
  static mean-price ratio, avoids stale-ratio drift as prices move.

Training (Meta-Labeling):
- Pair selection: Engle-Granger cointegration (p-value < 0.05) + Hurst
  Exponent (< 0.5) + out-of-sample ADF stationarity test.
- Architecture: LightGBM Classifier (n_estimators=200, max_depth=5,
  class_weight='balanced').
- Labeling: Lopez de Prado Triple Barrier Method — TP: Z→0, SL: |Z|≥4,
  Time Limit: 24h (96 candles).
- Entry Threshold: ML confidence > 60%.

Backtest:
- Baseline win-rate (raw statistical engine, no ML filter): 58.71%.
- ML-filtered win-rate: 88.67% out-of-sample (+5.60% absolute over the raw
  engine on the same period) — 150 trades validated / 431 opportunities
  proposed by the Z-Score engine.
- Retested on refreshed 180-day data (6 months later): raw win-rate dropped
  to 36.78%, ML-filtered backtest PnL turned negative (-6.74%) — an honest
  regime-dependence finding, not discarded, which directly motivated
  building the Regime Detector (Layer 0) instead of trusting a single
  static backtest indefinitely.

Deployment:
- Live on Binance Demo Trading (Futures), 15m scan cycle.
- Three independent stop-loss layers: Z-Score (|Z|≥6, black-swan protection),
  PnL (-5% of exposed capital, beta-mismatch protection — added after
  observing a live divergence where Z-Score improved while PnL kept
  deteriorating due to imperfect hedge coverage), Time Barrier (48h).
- Position recovery via live Binance API query on bot/service restart
  (handles Render redeploys without losing track of an open position).
- Weekly automated re-scan across 11 candidate pairs to adapt to structural
  market changes rather than trading a single hardcoded pair indefinitely.

Oversight:
- All trades, rejections (ML confidence < 60%), and stop-loss triggers
  logged to Supabase with full context (Z-Score, ML probability, hedge
  ratio, exit reason) and displayed on the public dashboard.
- Equity curve and win-rate tracked continuously against the backtest
  baseline to detect live/backtest drift early.

LAYER 2 : FUNDING RATE CARRY — active in HIGH_VOL / NEUTRAL
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Research:
- Concept: Delta-neutral carry — Long Spot + Short Perpetual on the same
  asset. Profit derived purely from the funding rate paid by leveraged
  longs every 8h; zero directional exposure.
- Rationale for regime placement: unlike StatArb, carry does not depend on
  mean-reversion — it profits from persistent long-side leverage demand,
  which typically increases (not decreases) during high-volatility/stress
  periods, making it a natural complement to StatArb rather than a
  competitor for the same regime.

Data:
- Funding rate history (30-day, per-8h payments) via Binance Futures API,
  across a 20-symbol liquid perpetual universe.
- Spot vs Perpetual basis (price gap) checked at entry to assess
  slippage/convergence risk.

Features:
- Current funding rate & annualized APR.
- 30-day average funding rate & annualized APR.
- Positive-payment consistency (% of the last 30d payments that were
  positive) — a proxy for signal stability, not just average magnitude.

Training:
- No ML — deterministic filter, but preceded by an explicit fee/breakeven
  analysis before any code was written: round-trip cost (entry + exit,
  both legs, standard fee tier) = 0.30%; breakeven holding period =
  round_trip_fee / daily_funding_rate.
- Selection thresholds: 30-day APR > 4%, positive-payment consistency > 60%,
  current funding rate > 0 (do not enter against the prevailing flow).

Backtest:
- Applied the selection filter to the live 20-symbol universe at research
  time: only 1 of 20 symbols (UNI/USDT) cleared the bar — APR 30d 4.47%,
  80% positive-payment consistency, breakeven ≈ 24.5 days. Confirms the
  filter is restrictive by design, not cosmetic — most scanned funding
  spikes were recent-only and did not survive the 30-day consistency check.

Deployment:
- Checked every 8h at Binance funding payment timestamps (00:00 / 08:00 /
  16:00 UTC) — not continuously, consistent with the strategy's structurally
  longer holding horizon.
- Exit trigger: rolling average of the last 5 funding payments turns
  negative, OR 45-day maximum holding period reached — avoids over-reacting
  to a single negative payment in an otherwise favorable trend.
- Position state persisted to JSON to survive bot/service restarts.
- Daily automated re-scan of the funding universe (independent thread) to
  keep the selected pair current without blocking the live position loop.

Oversight:
- Funding collected per cycle, cumulative funding, and unrealized basis PnL
  logged to Supabase every 8h (including idle FLAT cycles, so the dashboard
  reflects "no opportunity found" as clearly as an active position).
- All entries/exits logged with basis %, funding APR at execution, and exit
  reason, displayed on the public dashboard.
