Phase 1 — BTC/USDT 4H Swing Trading (Abandoned)
Research & Strategy
Asset: BTC/USDT. Market Characteristics: 24/7/365 trading environment, heavily retail-driven, distinct cyclical regimes. Horizon: Swing Trading. Drivers: Funding Rates, on-chain flows, macroeconomic cyclicality. Capital: 100€ initial budget (Forward-testing via Binance Testnet). Costs: Binance Futures (Maker: 0.02% | Taker: 0.05%).
Data
Market: OHLCV. Alternative: Funding Rate, Fear & Greed Index. Macro: DXY, Equities (via yfinance). Excluded: Open Interest (historical data unavailable at required granularity).
Training
Phase 1 (Directional): XGBoost (outperformed LightGBM), 4H horizon predicting T+24H (rolling window), Optuna TPE tuning.
Phase 2 (Meta-Labeling): Random Forest Classifier. Triple Barrier Setup: PT = 1.5x Vol, SL = 1.0x Vol, TL = 24h.
Break-even required > 40.0% precision. Primary model alone yielded 33.25% (Unprofitable). Meta-Model reached 55.14% at 54.0% confidence threshold.
Backtest & The Pivot
The 4H market exhibited too much of a "Random Walk" with severe intrabar noise. Impossible at this scale to construct a robust, winning strategy without massive capital to absorb drawdowns.
Decision: Pivot to 15-minute BTC Up or Down binary contracts on Polymarket.
Phase 2 — Polymarket 15-Minute Binary Bot (Abandoned)
Research & Strategy
Asset: Polymarket BTC Up/Down Contracts. Horizon: 15 minutes. Advantage: Eliminates stop-loss hunting, slippage, and complex position sizing. The binary label resolves precisely at close[t+15] > close[t], providing a mathematically pristine environment for the algorithm.
Data
Memory: Binance OHLCV 1m API (Incremental updates). Execution: Polymarket Gamma API (Dynamic token IDs) & CLOB API (Order book matching).
Features
Engineered specific short-term inertia indicators: Rolling Volatility (60m std), Momentum (5m & 15m returns), Volume Surge (vs 4H mean), and RSI (14-period). Absolute prices were strictly excluded to prevent look-ahead bias.
Deployment
Autonomous Python architecture deployed on Render. Scheduled via APScheduler at hh:02, hh:17, hh:32, hh:47 UTC. Flask dashboard acting as a live terminal with real-time logging and dynamic risk shielding (abort if bid/ask spread > $0.05).
Live Diagnosis & The Pivot
Market microstructure and liquidity constraints on Polymarket invalidate high-frequency directional trading. Target market showed a spread of $0.98 — risking $0.99 to gain $0.01 requires a >99.1% win rate, making the strategy mathematically unviable.
Decision: Pivot to Statistical Arbitrage (Pairs Trading) on Binance Futures.
Phase 3 — Statistical Arbitrage + Regime Detector (Live Demo)
Concept & Strategy
Market Neutral approach (Pairs Trading). By identifying two strongly co-integrated assets (AAVE/ETH), the algorithm trades the "spread" (mathematical divergence between them) rather than attempting to predict absolute price direction. Zero directional market exposure.
Statistical Foundation
Pair selection via Engle-Granger cointegration test (p-value < 0.05) + Hurst Exponent (< 0.5 = mean-reversion) + ADF out-of-sample stationarity test. Hedge ratio estimated dynamically via Rolling OLS (institutional standard) to avoid stale ratio drift.
ML Layer (Meta-Labeling)
A LightGBM classifier acts as a "gatekeeper" — only allowing entries when confidence > 60%. Features: Z-score momentum (1h/4h), volatility regime, directional returns. Trained via Lopez de Prado's Triple Barrier Method (TP=Z→0 / SL=Z≥4 / Time=24h). Baseline win-rate: 58.7% → ML-filtered: 88.67%.
Regime Detector
A two-level macro filter runs every hour: BTC realized volatility detects systemic stress (HIGH_VOL → funding carry activated), while ETH Hurst + ADX detects the microstructure regime (MEAN_REV → stat arb / TRENDING → momentum). Validated via walk-forward across 3 distinct market periods with Hurst separation of 0.21+ between regimes.
Risk Management
Three independent stop-loss layers: Z-Score SL (Z ≥ ±6.0, black swan), PnL SL (-5% of exposed capital, beta mismatch protection), Time Barrier (48h maximum holding, mean-reversion timeout). Position recovery on restart via live Binance API query.
Architecture
Fully autonomous Python pipeline: fetch_data → build_features → train_model → backtest → live_bot. Weekly automated pair rescanning selects the best cointegrated pair from 11 candidates. Regime + pair selection written to JSON, consumed by the trading bot at each 15m cycle.
Current Status
Running live on Binance Demo (Futures Paper Trading). Regime: MEAN_REV (ETH Hurst 0.27). Stat arb active on AAVE/ETH. Data persisted to Supabase, dashboard auto-refreshes every 30s.
